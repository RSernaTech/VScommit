Life Through My Scrapbook

Welcome to Life Through My Scrapbook, a creative space inspired by my love for preserving memories, vintage designs, and the little moments that make life meaningful.

Every page is like turning a page in a scrapbook, featuring vintage-inspired navigation, warm colors, decorative stitching, and creative details that reflect my personality and appreciation for life's memories.

Pieces of My Creative Journey

  • Connecting pages to create a seamless browsing experience.
  • Bringing each page together through a shared stylesheet.
  • Exploring three vintage-inspired navigation designs.
  • Creating a nostalgic atmosphere through warm colors, decorative details, and typography.

Just like a scrapbook, every page tells a story. Explore each section and discover how creativity, meaningful details, and thoughtful design can bring memories to life, one page at a time.
